Additional observations:

1. The only medical image classifier present on disk is a **separate** project,
   `RTB/1-Projects/knee-mri-abnormality-detection/` (RSNA knee MRI abnormality detection,
   per-slice-attention model). It is unrelated to the proxy-sampling claims. Its
   `MODEL_PROVENANCE.md` records that no genuine real-data checkpoint ever materialized
   (production builder exits `REAL_CHECKPOINT_REQUIRED`); this is independent of C1–C3.
2. No demographic (skin-tone) annotations, no proxy-extraction code, no sampling
   implementation, no statistical tests, and no manuscripts (.tex/.docx/.md) related to
   C1–C3 were found. Git history in the RTB repository contains no commits referencing
   the study.
3. Conclusion: **C1–C3 cannot be reproduced, verified, or contradicted locally.** They are
   retained throughout this rebuild as *historical hypotheses*, clearly labeled, and are
   tested afresh under matched, leakage-safe protocols. No historical number is reported
   as a result of this study.

## Consequences for the rebuild

- The rebuild begins from a clean repository: `RTB/1-Projects/proxy-sampling-study/`.
- Every number in the rebuilt manuscript must trace to a saved experiment artifact
  (`results/**.json`) generated by code in this repository with a recorded seed,
  config hash, and data checksum.
- Historical claims are restated as hypotheses H1–H3 in `CHECKLIST.md` and are only
  referenced in the manuscript as "unverified prior claims", never as evidence.
